Critical Swing Trading Rule for Options:
  • Never buy Weekly Options for Swing Trades: In a 5-day swing hold, weekly options suffer catastrophic Theta decay (−40% to −60% of premium). Even if the market moves in your direction, premium decay can wipe out profits.
  • Always use Monthly Contracts (>15 to 30 Days to Expiry): Monthly contracts experience gentle theta decay (≤ 2% to 3% per day), ensuring that positive delta from a +500 to +1,500 point move completely overpowers time decay.
  • Use Bull Call / Bear Put Spreads or Futures if IV (India VIX) is elevated (>16), which completely hedges out theta decay.
